Safety Center

Next is the fastest way out of a bad pair. Use it without explaining yourself.

End stops the session and local capture when you are done.

Cam / Mic cut your outgoing feed. Cover the lens if you walk away.

Do not share
  • Full name, home or work address, school, or workplace on camera.
  • Phone numbers, social handles, or payment details in text chat.
  • Photos of minors. This site is for adults only.
  • Passwords, QR codes, or one-time codes that appear on screen.
Camera and room

Check what is behind you: mail, nameplates, maps, other people. Prefer a plain wall. On laptops, a webcam cover when idle is still a good habit.

If someone asks you to move the camera, undress, or send money, skip. That is not a conversation; it is a script.

If something is illegal

Disconnect. Preserve evidence only if a competent authority asks you to. Report crimes to local law enforcement. This portal cannot police every session in real time.
