About this site

Monkey is a simple random video chat portal

This site exists so two people who do not know each other can open a webcam conversation without creating an account. It is built as a small web portal: information pages on one side, the live chat room on the other.

The product is deliberately narrow. There are no friend lists, public profiles, coins, or “levels.” You join the queue, you talk or you skip, you leave.

What you get
  • A matching queue for random live video.
  • On-page text chat while a session is connected.
  • Mic and camera toggles, Next, and End.
  • Pages that explain safety, rules, privacy, and how the tech works.
What this is not

Monkey is not a dating app, a social network, or a customer-support desk. People you meet are strangers. Treat every session as public until proven otherwise: do not show IDs, bank screens, workplace badges, or anything you would not want copied.

The name “Monkey” is used here as this project’s brand. It is not a claim of affiliation with any other company or app that uses a similar name.
