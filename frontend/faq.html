Frequently asked questions

FAQ

What is Monkey?

A free random video chat website. Two browsers are paired for a live webcam session. It is often used as a simple Omegle-style alternative.

Do I need an account?

No. Open Video Chat and click Start. No email, no password.

Is it free?

Yes. Entering the matching queue does not require payment.

Is it safe?

Media is intended to travel peer-to-peer, which means this project is not built as a call recorder. Strangers can still behave badly or capture their own screen. Read the Safety Center. You must be 18+.

How do I skip someone?

Click Next. That ends the current pair and looks for another person.

Does it work on phones?

In a modern mobile browser with camera permission, usually yes. Serve the site over HTTPS. There is no separate store app required.

Why is my camera blank?

Check the browser permission icon, close other apps using the webcam, try another browser, and confirm you are on HTTPS if the prompt never appears.

Why am I waiting a long time?

Matching needs a second person in the queue. Traffic varies. Stay on the page; switching tabs can pause some browsers.
