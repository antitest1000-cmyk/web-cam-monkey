Step by step

How a session is created

  1. You open the Video Chat page and click Start.
  2. The browser asks for camera and microphone permission. If you refuse, there is no usable video session.
  3. Your page joins a matchmaking queue on the signaling server.
  4. When another visitor is also waiting, both pages exchange connection details (SDP/ICE) through that server.
  5. Audio and video then try to flow directly between the two browsers using WebRTC.
  6. Next drops the current peer and returns you to the queue. End leaves the queue and stops local media.
What the server is for

The backend is a waiting room and a message relay for setup. It is not designed as a video recorder. That does not make a stranger on the other end trustworthy. They can still screenshot, record their own screen, or behave badly. Use Next.

Networks sometimes need extra help (NAT traversal). Connection quality depends on both machines, cameras, and internet links. HD is not guaranteed.

Browser requirements

Use a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari build. Camera access on some phones only works over HTTPS. If the site is served over plain HTTP on a LAN, permission prompts may fail.
