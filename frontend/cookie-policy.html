Website policy

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026. This page describes the current website build. Hosting, browser, and backend services can change, so review it when those services change.

Cookies and local storage

The current frontend does not include advertising or analytics scripts and does not intentionally set a cross-site advertising profile. The chat uses a real-time Socket.IO connection and WebRTC. Your browser and service providers may process technical identifiers, connection data, or storage needed to deliver those services.

Camera and microphone

Camera and microphone access is requested by your browser only when you choose to start a chat. You can revoke permission in your browser settings. Camera and microphone permissions are controlled by your device and browser, not by a cookie banner on this site.

Third-party infrastructure

The site is hosted on Netlify and the signaling service is hosted separately. The browser's WebRTC configuration uses public STUN servers for connection setup. Those providers may receive technical network requests under their own terms and privacy notices. This site does not control their logs or retention.

Changes

If analytics, advertising, or additional storage is added, this policy should be updated before that feature is used. See also the Privacy Policy.
