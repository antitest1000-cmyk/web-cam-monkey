Simple Safety Habits for Random Video Chat

A live video conversation can reveal more than you intend. A few checks before and during a chat help protect your privacy and make it easier to leave when something feels wrong.

Check the frame before you connect

Check the frame before you connect

Look around the camera view for mail, school or work badges, street signs, screens, family photos, and reflections that reveal where you are. A plain background gives away less. Headphones can keep other people nearby from being heard.

Keep identifying details private

Keep identifying details private

Avoid sharing your full name, phone number, home address, exact workplace or school, social accounts, passwords, one-time codes, or payment information. A stranger does not need those details for a friendly conversation.

Use the controls early

Use the controls early

You can end a conversation without explaining why. Use Next to move on or End to leave. Turn off your camera or microphone when you need a pause, and close the browser tab if the controls do not respond.

Remember that live media can be captured

Remember that live media can be captured

Peer-to-peer video does not stop the other person from recording their screen or using another device. Only show or say things you would be comfortable having seen again.

For adults only

For adults only

This service is intended for adults 18 and older. Do not involve minors, request or share sexual content involving minors, or continue a conversation where someone appears underage. Leave and report urgent threats to local authorities.

When a conversation crosses a line

When a conversation crosses a line

Disconnect, block the person through available browser or platform tools, and preserve only the information needed for a legitimate report. Do not retaliate or publish another person's private information. See our Community Guidelines and Safety Center.
