Community Guidelines

By entering the chat room you agree to these rules and to the Terms of use.

The rules
  1. 18+ only. Anyone under 18 is banned from using this site. Do not involve minors in any way.
  2. Lawful use only. No threats, scams, doxxing, or other crimes.
  3. No exploitation or abuse material. Zero tolerance.
  4. Consent. Do not pressure people on camera. Next is a complete answer.
  5. No spam or commercial blasting of links, tokens, or “investment” pitches.
  6. Respect the other person’s skip. Do not try to reconnect to harass someone who left.
Enforcement

This is a lightweight portal. Automated filters cannot see every peer-to-peer stream. If you break the law, that is a matter for authorities. Operators may still block IPs or take the service down.
