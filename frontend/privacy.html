Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026. This notice describes the current no-account website build. It is a plain-language summary, not a promise about third-party hosting logs or a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Information you provide

The public chat flow does not require an account or profile. Messages typed into chat are sent through the signaling service to the other participant while the session is active. The current backend code relays them and does not write chat history to a database. Do not send sensitive information to strangers.

Connection and device information

When the site loads or starts a chat, the browser and hosting providers necessarily process technical request and connection data. The backend logs socket connection events and temporary socket identifiers to its service log. The WebRTC setup uses public Google STUN servers, which may receive connection requests. Network addresses can be exposed during peer connection setup; peer-to-peer does not mean anonymous.

Camera and microphone

Your browser asks permission before the site can capture camera or microphone. Audio and video are intended to travel between browsers using WebRTC. The other participant can still record or capture what they see or hear. The site code does not provide a recording feature, but this cannot prevent recording by another person or third-party service logs.

Hosting providers

The frontend is hosted by Netlify and the signaling backend is hosted separately (currently configured for Render). These providers may process technical information under their own privacy terms and retention practices. The site operator does not control their infrastructure logs.

Cookies and tracking

The current frontend does not include analytics or advertising scripts. The browser and connection services may use technical identifiers or storage required to operate. See the Cookie Policy for more detail.

Children

This service is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. It is not designed for children, and minors must not use it.

Policy changes

Changes to the site or its providers may require updates to this notice. The date above shows the latest revision of this page. For current safety guidance, see our Safety Center and Community Guidelines.

Contact

The Contact page currently contains a demonstration form that does not transmit submissions. Do not send personal or sensitive information through it. A working privacy-request contact method needs to be configured by the site operator.
